In His Honour

A Note for You

Some words, written with love, on your special day.

Today marks another year of a life built with the same care you've brought to everything you do. As a civil engineer serving in the Pakistan Army, you've spent your career on foundations — the kind that hold bridges and structures steady, and the kind, quieter still, that hold a family together. Discipline, precision, and duty have always come naturally to you, but so has warmth.

We think of the lessons taught not through words alone, but through example — patience in hardship, humility in success, and love that asks for nothing in return. On your special day, we simply want you to know how deeply you are cherished.

May this new year bring you the same peace and steadiness you have given so generously to everyone around you.

With love and respect, always.

One Final Word

However far the years take us, this day will always belong to you.

Made with love, for a very special birthday 14 August — a day worth celebrating twice.
